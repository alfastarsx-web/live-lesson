# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for Claude Code (claude.ai/code) in this repository.

## User-facing text: tone of voice (mandatory)

Every message, page text, button, push, error and bot reply shown to students, mentors, operators or staff must follow [docs/TONE_OF_VOICE.md](docs/TONE_OF_VOICE.md) (the master copy lives in aiteacher-api): warm Uzbek courtesy ("siz", by name), short (2–4 lines, one idea), always add the brief reason/benefit, 1–2 emojis max, no pressure or bureaucratic tone, apologise and offer a fix on errors.
